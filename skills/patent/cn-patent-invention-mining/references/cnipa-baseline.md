# CNIPA baseline for software / AI invention drafting

This file is a working baseline. It is not a substitute for checking the currently effective law, implementing regulations, Patent Examination Guidelines, and official CNIPA notices before filing.

## Core drafting principles

### Technical solution framing

For software, algorithm, big-data, and AI inventions, analyze the claimed solution as a whole rather than mechanically separating algorithmic and technical features. Draft around interaction between the algorithm/software mechanism and technical features, the technical problem, and technical effect.

### Sufficient disclosure

When an invention materially concerns AI model construction or training, capture enough information regarding necessary model modules/layers/connections, training steps, and relevant parameters to enable implementation. For concrete technical domains, describe how model/algorithm input/output data relate to the technical scene.

### Inventive step

Do not rely on changing only an application object or scene when the underlying mechanism is unchanged and adaptation would be routine. Identify the technical adaptation, state relationship, parameter/mechanism change, or system interaction that causes a different technical effect.

## Official sources to verify

- CNIPA, `人工智能相关发明专利申请指引（试行）`, published 2024-12-31.
- CNIPA Order No. 84 modifying the Patent Examination Guidelines, published 2025-11-13 and effective 2026-01-01.

- Current Patent Law, Implementing Regulations, and effective Patent Examination Guidelines.

## Review reminder

Verify current rules, priority/public-disclosure dates, inventorship/ownership/confidentiality issues, and have final claims reviewed by a qualified Chinese patent professional.
