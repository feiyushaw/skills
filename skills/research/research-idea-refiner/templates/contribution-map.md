## Contribution overlap audit

For each pair of contribution bullets, ask whether one is merely an implementation detail or evidence for the other. Merge overlapping bullets.

## Reviewer-collapse test

Rewrite the method using the most standard terminology possible. If the contribution reduces to a known method plus a new application, revise the novelty claim.
