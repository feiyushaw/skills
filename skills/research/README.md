# Research Skills

The research stack preserves the lifecycle:

```text
Understand → Innovate → Prove → Communicate → Review
```

and the central constraint:

```text
Contribution → Claim → Required Evidence → Experiment / Analysis
→ Figure / Table → Paper Section
```

Translation and writing skills may improve language and discourse, but may not silently change claim strength, evidence status, scope, uncertainty, terminology, notation, or citations.
